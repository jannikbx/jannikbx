### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an **Office Manager** at [Munz Messtechnik](https://de.linkedin.com/company/munz-messtechnik), a certified service partner of ista SE in the field of modern measurement technology and transparent consumption billing (heat & water).

With a background in **educational work**, **regional management** (P.A.C. Schweiz GmbH), and a deep passion for **IT strategy, machine learning, and IT service management**, I bridge the gap between operational excellence and digital transformation.

-   🏢 **Currently:** Büromanager @ Munz Messtechnik *(since May 2025)*
-   📍 **Based in:** Laufenburg / Bad Säckingen, Germany
-   🎓 **Background:** Educator (Erzieher) with a strong tech affinity
-   🌱 **Learning:** Advanced automation, cloud infrastructure, and AI toolchains
-   🔎 **Status:** Open for interesting projects & collaborations
